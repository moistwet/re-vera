Hawker stall rents to rise 40% next year, vendors say

Wire report · Republished on Yahoo · 2 hours ago

Hawker stall rents across the island are set to rise by 40% from 1 January, according to vendors who spoke to reporters this week.

More than 200 stalls have already closed this year, the vendors said, blaming rising costs.

The last islandwide rent adjustment took effect in 2024.

A recent survey found that eight in ten hawkers are considering leaving the trade.

An official spokesperson was quoted as saying rents “will not be capped under any circumstances.”

The review takes effect on 1 January.
